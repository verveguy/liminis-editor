#902's double-hard-break marker serializes as a line containing only the
two-space hard-break notation (see the "A note on byte-identity" section of
README.md). CommonMark treats a whitespace-only line as blank regardless of
trailing spaces, so re-parsing this fixture's first-pass output splits the
paragraph instead of preserving the marker on a second pass. This is the
pre-existing hard-break trailing-backslash-to-trailing-spaces normalization
that #943's Out of Scope section explicitly excludes from the idempotence
guarantee.
